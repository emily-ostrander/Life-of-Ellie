## About the Collection

This is a collection representing the life of Ellie. Ellie is a black labrador, golden retriever mixed dog. She was born in 2011 on a farm in Tipton, Iowa. She had 12 brothers and sisters. Ellie came to live with the McQuiston family in May of 2011. Throughout the collection you will see Ellie age as our family grows.
